Zero your scoped rifle, step by step

Nullpunkt is a free web app for hunters and sport shooters who want to zero a rifle with a scope or check its point of impact. You print a target, photograph it after shooting, and the app tells you how many clicks to turn on your scope: in mrad or MOA, dead on or with a hunting zero (for example 4 cm high at 100 m).

What Nullpunkt does

  • Evaluates your group from a photo: group size, mean point of impact and the offset in centimetres and clicks. The corner markers on the target keep the photo true to scale, even when taken at an angle.
  • Detects flyers: such as the clean-bore shot after cleaning. You decide which hits count towards the group.
  • Measures steadiness before the shot: the smartphone on the rifle uses its motion sensors to record how still the rifle was in the second before the shot. You see whether the spread comes from the rifle or from the shooter.
  • Assesses the confirmation shot: after adjusting, Nullpunkt checks whether the shot lands inside the group circle around the target point.
  • Saves setups: several rifles, scopes and loads, with history.

Who it is for

For hunters before the season, after changing a mount or when the scope took a knock. For sport shooters and reloaders testing a new load. For anyone who doesn’t want to guess when zeroing.

How it works

  1. Create a setup: rifle, calibre, scope with click value, ammunition.
  2. Print the target and attach the smartphone firmly to the scope or stock with a flexible phone tripod.
  3. Record a rest reference: rifle on the rest, don’t touch anything.
  4. Fire a group of 5 shots and photograph the hits.
  5. Check for flyers, turn the correction on the scope.
  6. Fire a confirmation shot and get it confirmed.

Runs in the browser on iPhone and Android, no sign-up, no app store. All data stays on your device.
